Shot over three nights on the Long Beach waterfront, "Midnight Harbor" leans on
practical sodium-vapor light and handheld 35mm to build a slow-burn noir mood.
The lighting plan used only sources that existed in the location — dock lamps,
truck headlights, a single tungsten practical inside the foreman's office —
to keep performances grounded and the camera free to move.
